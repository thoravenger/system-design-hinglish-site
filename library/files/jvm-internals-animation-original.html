JVM internals / dekhkar samjho
← Features page

Object banta kahan hai, badalta kaise hai, marta kab hai.

Paanch animations. Har ek mein Play dabao ya step-by-step aage badho — neeche har step ka matlab Hinglish mein likha hai. Ye wahi cheezein hain jo interview mein "internally kya hota hai" poochne par expected hoti hain: heap allocation aur GC, virtual threads ka mount/unmount, stream ka lazy execution, record/sealed/pattern matching ka compile-to-runtime safar, aur ek object ka poora janm-mrityu chakra.

Ye simplified models hain — asli JVM mein TLABs, card tables, remembered sets, safepoints aur bahut kuch aur hai. Yahan sirf wo hissa dikhaya hai jo design decisions samajhne ke liye kaafi hai.
