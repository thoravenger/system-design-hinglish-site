Kaise padhein — How to use this site

Ye site ek course ki tarah design ki gayi hai. Random order mein bhi padh sakte ho, lekin ye order sabse zyada fayda dega.

1

Foundations pehle (01–07)

Scaling, estimation, framework, rate limiter, consistent hashing, KV store, ID generator — ye "LEGO blocks" hain. Baaki har chapter inhe reuse karta hai. Chapter 03 (framework) ko do baar padho.

2

Classic designs (08–14)

URL shortener, crawler, notifications, feed, chat, autocomplete, YouTube — sabse zyada pooche jaane wale problems. Har ek ko 45 min ke timer ke saath whiteboard pe khud draw karo.

3

Vol 2 deep systems (15–28)

Location (16–18), infra (19–21, 24), transactions/fintech (22, 26–28). Senior / lead / FDE interviews ke liye. Apne domain ke chapters pehle uthao.

4

Har chapter ka routine

Hero + Key Takeaways pehle padho → diagrams ke captions follow karo → "Interview mein kaise bolein" callout → follow-up questions khud answer karke phir kholo.

4-step interview framework (Chapter 03 ka summary)

StepKya karna haiTime (45 min)
1. Problem samjho & scopeClarifying questions: features, users/scale, platforms, constraints. Assumptions board pe likho. Seedha solution mein mat kudo.3–10 min
2. High-level design + buy-inAPIs, box diagram (client → LB → services → cache/DB/queue), back-of-envelope numbers. Interviewer ko teammate maan ke agreement lo.10–15 min
3. Deep dive2–3 critical components: data model, sharding, hot paths, consistency, failure handling. Trade-offs bolo.10–25 min
4. Wrap-upBottlenecks, failure scenarios, monitoring, 10x scale pe kya badlega, recap.3–5 min
Callouts ka matlab💡 Tip = shortcut/insight · ⚠️ Warn = failure mode ya common galti · 🎤 Interview = 45-min mein kaise drive karein · 🌍 Real world = production systems mein ye kaise dikhta hai.
